# Commerce MCP overview

[!BADGE Coming Soon]{type=Informative tooltip="This feature is coming soon and is subject to change."}

The Commerce MCP for Adobe Commerce helps developers build custom agentic experiences from discovery to checkout using real-time commerce data and tools. In this video, a custom AI Assistant uses the Commerce MCP to turn conversations into conversions by understanding shopper intent and delivering relevant product recommendations.

## Who is this video for?

* Commerce store owners
* Merchandisers
* Digital marketers

## Video content

* Conversational product discovery using natural language instead of filters and search bars
* Context-aware session management retaining cart contents and shopper preferences
* Bidirectional cart management by typing, with no page reloads or confirmation modals
* Checkout completed within the conversation, without navigating away
* Post-purchase contextual guidance specific to the product purchased
