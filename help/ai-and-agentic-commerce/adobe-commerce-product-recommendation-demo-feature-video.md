# Adobe Commerce Product Recommendation Demo - Feature Video

Adobe Commerce enables users to add Adobe Sensei-based product recommendations to any storefront page. This demo shows how to configure a "More Like This" recommendation on the product detail page (PDP), including placement, count, and stock or price rules.

## Who is this video for?

* Merchandisers
* Commerce administrators

## Video content

* Adding a "More Like This" product recommendation to the product detail page (PDP)
* Configuring recommendation name, placement, product count, and display order
* Setting price-range inclusion rules relative to the current product price
* Excluding out-of-stock and low-stock products, with a configurable stock threshold
* Activating the recommendation and confirming it appears on the PDP
